INTERPRETATION
================================================================================

The Greedy approach significantly outperforms ODE because it successfully
identifies and commits to the highest-impact edge first (bg--fi: +€57.9M).
This creates a market equilibrium that makes subsequent edges more valuable.

ODE's price adjustment dynamics prevent it from recognizing bg--fi's full
potential, leading to a sub-optimal first choice (dk--fr: +€11.7M). This
missing opportunity cascades through the remaining iterations.

Interestingly, both approaches add edges with 0 or negative gains in final
rounds - but this is just budget exhaustion filling available capacity.
The key value is captured in the first 6 iterations for Greedy and first 
6-10 for ODE, but Greedy's early focus on high-value edges compounds better.
